# Children of Morta — tear-down

**What it is:** Story-wrapped action-roguelite: a family hub (Bergsons' house)
frames runs; per-run power comes from family talents; the *narrative* — not the
build — is what drags you through dozens of failed runs.

**Why it works:** The hub is emotional glue: you return to faces, not menus.
Short failures are cushioned by story beats, converting rage-quit energy into
"one more". Meta upgrades are legible and cheap early.

**Implementation likelihood:** hub scene with persistent NPCs; meta talent
grid; story snippets doled at run boundaries.

**In HOLLOW SUN terms:** our hub is **the shrine at the heart of the dead star**
— the rekindled sun IS the story meter. Meta purchases visibly stoke the sun on
the title screen. Dawn embers = narrative progress ("the star remembers you").

**We deliberately differ:** no character switching; one ember, one identity.
Story is environmental (the sun, the shells, biome names) — no dialogue overhead
for a one-developer scope.
