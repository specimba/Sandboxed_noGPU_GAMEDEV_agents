# Hades — tear-down

**What it is:** Isometric action roguelite where between-biome boons (god
gifts) build the run; death returns you to a hub with mirror-of-night meta
upgrades; "death is content" — every run-end advances relationships/story.

**Why it works:** Boons are readable, front-loaded power spikes with clear
rarity tiers; the hub gives death a *function*; difficulty pacts let strong
players self-tune. Escape attempts have a clear top goal (the surface).

**Implementation likelihood:** boon choice UI (3 cards, rerolls); mirror meta
tree (flat cost unlocks, then rank-ups); heatmap of player death points.

**In HOLLOW SUN terms:** boon cards after each room = Hades gifts; **the
Shrine of Dawn = Mirror of Night** (dawn embers, one-time unlocks with ranks).
Clear top goal: reach THE HEART and rekindle the sun fully — the win screen is
the sun igniting (streamable climax).

**We deliberately differ:** no run-lengthening pacts yet; no dialogue systems —
our "death is content" is the shrine + the sun staying brighter between runs.
