# Hollow Knight — tear-down

**What it is:** Metroidvania whose difficulty is *legibility*: every enemy and
hazard telegraphs in silhouette, sound, and pause. Currency (geo) drops on
death and can be re-claimed — risk stays emotionally loaded but recoverable.
Silence and space carry atmosphere.

**Why it works:** Deaths read as "I understood the pattern too late", never
"the game cheated". The world itself (landmarks, biomes) is the progress bar.

**Implementation likelihood:** strict telegraph grammar (wind-up → flash →
act); shade-run mechanic analog (recover dropped geo).

**In HOLLOW SUN terms:** our telegraph grammar: striker line flash, warden
spawn ring, spawn-mark shrink. Elite variants must speak the same grammar (a
*ring* = shield, trail = swift). Dawn embers never drop on death — we keep
losses clean; instead the **sun brightness persists across runs** (our shade =
a permanently warmer star).

**We deliberately differ:** no sprawling map — one arena, mastered depth-first;
no charm-slot puzzle: boon slots are unlimited but visually stacked in HUD.
